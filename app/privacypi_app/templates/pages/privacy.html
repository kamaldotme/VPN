Make every device use PrivacyPi's ad-blocking

Checking…

Some devices and apps (smart TVs, some phones and browsers) try to skip PrivacyPi's ad and tracker blocker by using their own lookup service. Turn this on to send all of them through PrivacyPi's blocker anyway. If an app stops working afterwards, turn it off again.

Keep clock checks private

Checking…

Your devices regularly ask servers on the internet what time it is. Those requests reveal that the device exists and when it is switched on. Turn this on and PrivacyPi answers the time itself, so the requests never leave your home.

Strengthen the WiFi security

Switches PrivacyPi's WiFi to the strongest protection its WiFi hardware supports: devices on the WiFi are kept apart from each other, and — if the hardware can do it — the newest WiFi security (WPA3) and protection against being kicked off the WiFi by an attacker are turned on.

Sign in to hotel, café or airport WiFi

Checking…

Public WiFi often shows a sign-in page before it lets you online. PrivacyPi's protections block that page. If PrivacyPi gets its internet from such a WiFi, pause the protections for 30 minutes, sign in, and they switch back on by themselves.

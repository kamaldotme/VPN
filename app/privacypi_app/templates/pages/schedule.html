Your schedule

Each rule says: between these times, on these days, use this privacy mode. If two rules cover the same time, the one higher up in the list wins. Outside all rules, your normal mode is used.

Ideas

  • Extra privacy at night: Tor from 22:00 to 07:00, every day.
  • Full speed during work: Normal internet from 09:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday.
  • Bedtime for the internet: Block all internet from 23:00 to 06:00.

If the end time is earlier than the start time, the rule runs past midnight into the next morning.

How it works

  1. Install the free WireGuard app on your phone or laptop (from the App Store, Google Play or wireguard.com).
  2. Add the device below. PrivacyPi shows a QR code — in the WireGuard app choose “Add tunnel” → “Scan QR code”.
  3. When you are away from home, switch the tunnel on in the app.

One-time step on your home router: it must pass incoming connections to PrivacyPi. Look for “port forwarding” in the router's settings and forward the UDP port shown after “Endpoint” in the setup text (normally 51820) to PrivacyPi.

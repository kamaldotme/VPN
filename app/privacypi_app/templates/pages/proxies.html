What is this for?

A proxy is another computer that passes your traffic on, so websites see its address instead of yours. It helps where VPNs are blocked, or when you rent an address in a particular country. Most people do not need one — a VPN is simpler.

Three steps: 1. add a proxy below · 2. press “Use this proxy” · 3. choose Proxy on the Routing page.

Where to send alerts

Add a place where PrivacyPi should send messages — for example a Telegram chat, a Discord channel, the ntfy app, or an email address. PrivacyPi needs internet to send them.

What is a service link? Show examples

A service link tells PrivacyPi which app to use and where to deliver the message. Replace the CAPITAL parts with your own details:

  • ntfy app: ntfy://TOPIC
  • Telegram: tgram://BOT_TOKEN/CHAT_ID
  • Discord: discord://WEBHOOK_ID/WEBHOOK_TOKEN
  • Email (Gmail): mailto://USER:PASSWORD@gmail.com?to=YOU@example.com

More services are listed in the Apprise guide (opens an outside website).
